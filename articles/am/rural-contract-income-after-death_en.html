Unpaid Mainland rural contract income: should a Macau heir check the contract or the building first?

If a Macau family knows that a relative held land in a Mainland village, do not describe it simply as a property transfer. The file may involve a rural land-contract right, a land-management right, unpaid contract income, or a building on the land. Record the village, contract, contracting party, income period and any transfer arrangement before choosing documents.

Key point first

If a Macau family knows that a relative held land in a Mainland village, do not describe it simply as a property transfer. The file may involve a rural land-contract right, a land-management right, unpaid contract income, or a building on the land. Record the village, contract, contracting party, income period and any transfer arrangement before choosing documents.

First question to settle

Preserve the contract, notices from the collective or contracting party, payment records and parcel information. Ask the local agriculture authority or contracting party what it currently requires. Contract income may be dealt with through succession, but continuation of a contract can depend on the contract type and collective-membership facts.
