A company share certificate is kept in Macau: which two legal subjects should a Mainland China estate file separate?

A share certificate kept in Macau may be an important lead, but it does not settle every question. Keep separate the person entitled to deal with the estate and the interest shown in the Mainland company’s records; otherwise the family can confuse an inheritance question with a company-record question.

2026-09-13

What the certificate can, and cannot, show

A certificate may show a company name, contributor, date, seal or a trace of a past change. Preserve every page and record where it was found and who held it. That creates a useful trail. It does not normally prove that the stated percentage remains current, or that a family member may now dispose of an estate interest.

For a Mainland China company, file the company location, unified social credit code if known, articles, shareholder register, funding papers and transfer papers separately from the certificate. If the paper and current company record do not match, mark the difference for checking rather than choosing one as the answer.

One file asks who may act for the estate

Death records, family relationship records, a will, any renunciation and the possible existence of other heirs address heirship. The date, name format and relationship chain in Macau documents can affect how a Mainland receiving party reviews them. A practical file distinguishes confirmed facts, family recollection and documents whose originals are still missing.

That work may identify who can make an inquiry, give authority or take part in the process. It does not, by itself, decide the scope, value or transferability of a company interest.

A second file asks what company interest is actually in the estate

The deceased may have been the registered shareholder, the actual funder, a transferee, or simply the person keeping the papers. There may also have been capital changes, a transfer, a pledge, preservation measure, distributions or a control dispute. Establish whether an interest is still within the estate before deciding who succeeds to it.

Where the Mainland company interest or connected estate claim would ordinarily involve RMB 200,000 or more, this distinction has real commercial weight. Value still needs to be assessed from company, transaction and dispute records; neither the certificate face value nor a family estimate is enough.

The company has its own record and its own receiving questions

Even with a working heirship picture, the company side may require its articles, current shareholder information, past resolutions and location-specific requirements to be checked. A missing document is different from competing claims, withheld company information or an imminent disposal of value; the next step changes with the problem.

Do not assume that a Macau document enables immediate registration with a Mainland company. First specify the purpose: an information request, copies, confirmation of the register, participation in distribution, or a response to a dispute.

A short list before sending the file

Keep the original certificate and a complete image set. Note the company name, location and any known unified social credit code. Sort death, relationship, will and authority material by source. Flag who holds originals, who may disagree, and whether transfer, pledge, enforcement or time pressure is known. That gives a Mainland receiving party or lawyer a usable starting point without promising an outcome.
