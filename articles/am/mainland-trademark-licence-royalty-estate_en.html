Key point first

If a deceased person owned a Mainland trademark and received brand-licence royalties, a Macau family should not start with bank statements alone. Confirm the registered owner, licence agreement, payment cycle, licensee and any filing or amendment. The mark, contractual income and a company’s right to use the brand may belong to different parties.

First question to settle

Keep the registration number, licence and supplemental agreements, invoices, statements and payment notices. Put the contract term and date of death on one timeline. Until the rights holder or estate administrator is clear, do not promise renewal, a new bank account or a waiver of collection. Confirm translation and authentication for Macau documents with the actual Mainland receiving party. See company equity inheritance and document handover.
