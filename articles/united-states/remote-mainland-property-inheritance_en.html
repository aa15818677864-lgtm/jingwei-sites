United States and Mainland legal matters

Can a U.S.-based family inherit Mainland property entirely remotely?

Some searches, document preparation and agency steps can proceed remotely, but personal attendance depends on location, route, document status and family dispute.

Last updated: 2026-08-18

Three practical moves

  • Check registration, occupation, mortgage and all heirs.
  • Confirm attendance, video or agency requirements with the actual recipient.
  • Prepare U.S. documents by purpose and reserve major decisions.

Start with the actual decision

Break 'fully remote' into search, succession confirmation, signature, filing, collection, sale and receipt of proceeds, and ask about each separately.
