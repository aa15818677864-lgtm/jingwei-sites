Mainland property inheritance for Hong Kong families

A Mainland home has a registered right of residence: what heirs should check

Heirs may acquire ownership of a home while a registered right of residence continues to affect its use. When the residence-right holder dies, that personal right generally ends under Mainland law. Check the register and contract, then address ownership and residence separately.

Published: 2026-10-23

List the owner and residence-right holder separately

Check whether the register records a residence right, its holder, term and supporting contract. The owner’s death and the residence-right holder’s death are different events. A change of owner alone does not establish that the right has ended.

Check the term and cancellation documents

The Civil Code says a residence right cannot be transferred or inherited and ends when its term expires or its holder dies. Cancellation still follows registry procedure. Before a sale or later registration, confirm what death evidence and application the local office requires.

Keep ownership and occupation clauses distinct

A will can dispose of the testator’s own property interest, but creating or preserving residence arrangements raises separate questions about the holder, term, registration and co-owners. State who receives ownership separately from who may live there and for how long.

Documents and points to check

  • Current real estate register and residence-right entry.
  • The residence-right agreement or will clause creating it.
  • Identity of the holder, owner and any co-owners.
  • The term and evidence of death or expiry.
  • The registry’s instructions for cancellation or later transfer.
