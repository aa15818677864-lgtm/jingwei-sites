Mainland property inheritance for Hong Kong families

Tracking mortgage payments on a Mainland estate property: a guide for Hong Kong families

Several relatives may keep a deceased owner's mortgage paid while inheritance is being resolved. Without a common record, it becomes difficult to distinguish a temporary advance from an agreed contribution. Reconcile the loan, the family payments and the mortgage registration as separate matters.

Published: 2026-10-23

Establish the loan position and each person's capacity

Obtain the available loan contract and identify the borrower, any co-borrower and guarantor. Ask the bank what an authorised person needs to obtain a current statement covering principal, interest and any arrears. A debit appearing on an account proves a payment, not that the whole loan has been settled.

Mainland inheritance rules generally limit an heir's responsibility for the deceased's debts to the actual value inherited. That is a different question from a family member's own liability as a co-borrower, guarantor or under a separate valid undertaking. Renouncing an inheritance should not be treated as an answer to every contractual obligation.

Keep both a payment ledger and a family agreement

For each payment, record the payer, date, amount, destination, relevant loan and evidence of credit to the loan account. If money travels from Hong Kong to a Mainland account before repayment, preserve both stages. A transfer description is useful evidence of purpose, but does not establish that everyone accepted the arrangement.

Separately record whether the money is an advance, a gift or a contribution that the payer has agreed to bear. Identify who approved it and how reimbursement is intended to work. Paying instalments does not, by itself, increase an heir's property share. Keep disputed contributions visible rather than deducting them silently from the distributable estate.

Follow settlement through to registration

Before early repayment, confirm the bank's documentation requirements, official payment channel and the date for which its settlement figure is valid. Check for other mortgages, court restrictions and co-ownership issues; clearing one loan may leave other matters outstanding.

After obtaining confirmation of settlement, establish who must apply for mortgage cancellation and what evidence of completion will be issued. Check the updated register and file it with the settlement documents. A representative's authority should distinguish enquiries, repayment coordination, mortgage cancellation and inheritance registration. Authority to sell or receive sale proceeds needs its own review.

Payment reconciliation file

  • Loan agreement and bank-confirmed balance.
  • Original payment and loan-credit records.
  • Written treatment of advances and reimbursement.
  • Verified settlement figure and payment channel.
  • Settlement confirmation and updated mortgage status.
  • Representative's powers and unresolved items.
