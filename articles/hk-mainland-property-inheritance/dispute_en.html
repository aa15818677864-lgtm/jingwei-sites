Hong Kong residents inheriting Mainland property: what if heirs disagree or refuse to cooperate?

Last updated: 2026-07-22

When heirs do not cooperate, do not start by guessing the procedure. First identify the Mainland city, the heir list, and whether the blockage is signing, documents, the will or the distribution plan.

Start with the blockage

If agreement is possible, fix the documents first. If not, consider the court route.

Notary and registration offices usually do not resolve substantive family disputes.

Common blockages

  • Someone refuses to sign or disagrees over shares.
  • Heirs are in Hong Kong, overseas or different Mainland cities.
  • Someone is missing or refuses identity or relationship documents.
  • A will is challenged, or another possible heir may have been omitted.

Usual route

  1. Confirm the property city, registered owner and certificate status.
  2. Organise death, relationship, marriage, will or declaration documents.
  3. If heirs cooperate, proceed with notarisation, authorisation or registration supplements.
  4. If they do not, preserve evidence and assess inheritance confirmation or estate division litigation.

When court action may be needed

If an heir clearly objects, remains missing, challenges the will, or the notary/registration route is stuck, the key issue is usually to confirm who inherits and how the estate is divided.

Prepare first

  • Property certificate, real estate certificate or purchase contract.
  • Death certificate, marriage record, birth record or relationship proof.
  • Chat records, lawyer letters, refusal reasons, will or declaration.
  • Mortgage, seizure, co-owner or missing-certificate status.
