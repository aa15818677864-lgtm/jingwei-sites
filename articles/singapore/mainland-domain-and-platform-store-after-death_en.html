A Mainland domain and online shop use one identity: separate accounts from estate assets

If a Singapore family finds that a deceased person’s Mainland domain, online shop and payout account use the same identity, do not assume they are one inheritable asset. A domain is an address; a shop may contain inventory, receivables, deposits, customer records and a platform contract. The payout account is a separate verification trail.

Key point first

If a Singapore family finds that a deceased person’s Mainland domain, online shop and payout account use the same identity, do not assume they are one inheritable asset. A domain is an address; a shop may contain inventory, receivables, deposits, customer records and a platform contract. The payout account is a separate verification trail.

First question to settle

Preserve the registration record, platform messages, balance, orders and dispatch records. Separate personal, company and platform operator details. Do not change the phone binding or transfer the balance to a family member’s account first. The platform’s identity and risk rules must be checked alongside the succession documents.
