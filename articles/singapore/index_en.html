Singapore families handling an estate in Mainland China

Start with the will, the person authorised to represent the estate, and the Mainland city where each property, account or company interest is located. Keep the Singapore court file separate from the asset file.

Will and court grantsMainland assetsFamily positions

Separate the Singapore process from the asset file

  1. With a will, locate the original and named executor.
  2. Without a valid will, identify who may apply to administer the estate.
  3. Make a separate list of Mainland property, accounts and shares.
  4. Confirm document format, translation and overseas-use requirements.
