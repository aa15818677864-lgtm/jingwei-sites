Singapore and Mainland legal matters

Keep Singapore and Mainland teams on one bilingual case record

Do not let two language files evolve separately. Maintain one factual source and stable document identifiers, then explain the same facts naturally for each audience.

Last updated: 2026-08-18

Three practical moves

  • Create a fixed bilingual glossary for people, entities, contracts and exhibits.
  • Update the factual source before synchronising summaries and task lists.
  • Track translation questions by version, owner and update time.

Start with the actual decision

Names, dates, amounts, roles and document numbers must trace back to originals. Narrative can change with language; facts cannot drift silently.

Separate confirmed facts, open questions and the outcome the family or business wants. That allows the Mainland handler to decide whether investigation, document preparation, negotiation, registration or a formal procedure comes next.
