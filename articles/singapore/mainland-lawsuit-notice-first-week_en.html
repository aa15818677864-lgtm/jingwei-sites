Received Mainland litigation papers in Singapore? Use the first week to protect the file

The first week is for preserving deadlines, verifying delivery, confirming case identity and centralising evidence, not rushing into a long response.

Last updated: 2026-08-18

Three practical moves

  • Preserve envelopes, messages, platform records and every attachment.
  • Verify court, case number, parties, claims and each stated date.
  • Appoint one Singapore contact and one Mainland handler for version control.
