Article / Overseas heirs and Mainland estate funds

Several overseas heirs will receive Mainland estate funds: check recipients and the allocation basis

When overseas heirs need to distribute Mainland estate funds to people in several countries, separate three questions: who is entitled to receive, what document supports the allocation, and what each bank will accept. Keep four columns for the heir list, allocation basis, Mainland payment-out record and each recipient account. Key point first

When overseas heirs need to distribute Mainland estate funds to people in several countries, separate three questions: who is entitled to receive, what document supports the allocation, and what each bank will accept. Keep four columns for the heir list, allocation basis, Mainland payment-out record and each recipient account. Avoid having one family member receive everything under an informal promise to forward it.

First question to settle

If someone collects on behalf of others, keep the authority and delivery record separately. Reconcile the RMB amount, fees, exchange rate and amount received. Explain English, Chinese and account-name differences before a bank asks. Confirm the current remittance and compliance route with the actual banks.
