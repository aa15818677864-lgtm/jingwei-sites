After a Mainland Retiree Dies, How Do Pension Payments, Account Balances, and Survivor Benefits Differ?

An unpaid monthly pension, an individual pension-account balance, and benefits eligible survivors may claim are different categories. Confirm the person’s scheme and contribution location, then ask the local social-security office about each item separately.

Published: 2026-10-23

Monthly basic pension payments stop

Separate amounts paid before death, sums due for an earlier period, and later credits. Shenzhen’s human-resources authority says a basic pension is no longer paid after a retiree dies. If money arrives later, ask the local office which month it covers, whether it must be returned, and what process applies. A bank credit alone does not establish that the family may distribute it.

Death reporting and suspension procedures vary. Prepare the death certificate, identity details, and social-security card or number. Ask whether the report can be filed online, who may apply, and whether the former employer must assist.

The individual-account balance differs from survivor benefits

For a participant in employee basic pension insurance, the individual-account balance may be inherited under law. The local office calculates the payable amount and identifies proof of identity and succession relationship. This account balance is different from a monthly basic pension that stopped at death.

Eligible survivors may separately apply for funeral assistance and a one-time survivor pension. Eligibility, calculation, and evidence depend on the scheme, contribution period, time already receiving benefits, and local rules. Do not apply a figure from another province. Ask the office to identify the claimant, destination account, and required records for each benefit.

Families abroad should identify the right office

Use the retirement certificate, social-security card, benefit notices, former employer, and bank records to identify the contribution location and scheme. List prior payments, the individual-account balance, any possible overpayment after death, and survivor benefits separately. Keep a written or downloadable record of the office’s answer.

The relative who contacts the office is not automatically entitled to collect everything alone. Confirm the heirs, the applicant accepted by the office, and whether other survivors must authorise or provide records. Ask how overseas documents must be prepared for Mainland China. Different pension schemes and retiree categories can have different claimants, calculations, and documents. If the deceased worked in several places or transferred social-insurance coverage, first identify the office responsible for the final benefit and the individual account. This helps avoid sending an application to the wrong location. If the family receives an unexplained payment, record its date, description, and amount and ask the administering office to identify it before returning money to an individual or third party.

Ask the social-security office about

  • Contribution location, scheme, and social-security number
  • Payment-stop month and any later credit
  • Inheritable account balance and applicant documents
  • Local eligibility and standards for survivor benefits
