Article / Overseas heirs and Mainland procedure

An overseas heir receives Mainland court material electronically: preserve service evidence and time zones

When an overseas heir receives electronic material said to come from a Mainland court, arbitration body or lawyer, preserve the source and time before deciding whether it is formal service. Keep the original email, attachments, text or platform notice, sender account, download time, case number and any stated date. Do not rely on a screenshot or calculate a deadline solely from the overseas time zone.

First question to settle

Record Beijing time beside local time, identify the addressee and the action requested, and ask whether remote handling is permitted. For several heirs, record who received what and when; family forwarding does not necessarily replace individual service. See remote Mainland counsel and Mainland court papers.
