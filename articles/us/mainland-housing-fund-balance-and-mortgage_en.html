A Mainland Housing-Fund Account Has a Balance and an Unpaid Mortgage. What Should Heirs Check?

The housing-fund balance and mortgage are separate matters. An heir or legatee may apply to withdraw a deceased employee’s balance under local rules. If the deceased was also a borrower, review the loan agreement, co-borrowers, and security separately.

Published: 2026-10-23

Confirm the account and local process

Contact the housing-fund centre where the deceased contributed. Ask whether the account is sealed, its balance, and required documents. The State Council’s Housing Provident Fund Regulations allow heirs and legatees to withdraw a deceased employee’s stored balance, but the application follows local procedure.

Beijing’s 2026 guide describes online and in-person routes and differentiates documents by applicant and balance. It is an example, not a national threshold. Ask the relevant centre about death, identity, relationship, will, or legacy records; whether the account must first be sealed or closed; and whether an overseas heir may appoint a representative.

The balance does not automatically repay a loan

A positive fund balance does not show the mortgage is repaid, and it does not mean the centre will transfer funds to the lender. Locate the loan agreement and latest statement. Identify the borrower, co-borrowers, guarantor, mortgaged home, outstanding balance, and missed payments. Ask the lender what happens after the borrower’s death.

If the home is also estate property, check title, mortgage, and guarantees. Article 1161 of the Civil Code limits an heir’s liability for the deceased person’s legally owed debts to the actual value of the estate received. Whether a family member has a contractual loan obligation depends on the loan and security documents; it cannot be inferred from a fund withdrawal.

A U.S.-based family can contact both institutions

Collect the contribution city, former employer, fund account, property records, and loan documents. Contact the fund centre and lender separately. If nobody can travel to Mainland China, confirm power-of-attorney, witness, notarisation, translation, and original-document requirements before sending identity or family records. Ask whether the representative must be a particular heir.

If heirs disagree about the balance, who should take the home, or repayment, avoid combining everything into a vague consent letter. List account assets, property rights, and loan obligations separately, then consider agreement, an administrative application, or court process. Jingwei Law handles Mainland China legal matters only. Local rules and each contract must be checked on their terms.

The housing-fund account and mortgage may be administered by different offices. A balance statement from the fund centre does not confirm the loan position, so obtain a separate loan statement from the lender. If there is a co-borrower, confirm that person’s contractual role and the repayment method. Do not stop payments or change a debit account based only on the borrower’s death. Request written instructions before changing an arrangement that affects the home or heirs.

Prepare separate inquiries

  • Contribution city, fund-account status, and balance
  • Death, identity, relationship, and succession records
  • Loan contract, borrowers, mortgage, and current balance
  • Separate questions for the fund centre and lender
