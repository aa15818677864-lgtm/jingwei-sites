Mainland copyright income was omitted from U.S. probate papers: what should happen next?

If U.S. probate papers were completed before the family discovered Mainland copyright royalties or platform revenue, the omission does not by itself decide ownership. Confirm the source of the payment, the contract counterparty, the payment period and whether the deceased held an inheritable economic right. The next step may be supplemental estate material, an enquiry to the payer or a dispute among heirs.

First question to settle

Keep licence agreements, statements, work versions, payment notices, U.S. estate documents and the heir list. Do not change the payout account or sign a new licence for the deceased first.
