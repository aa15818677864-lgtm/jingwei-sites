Macau and Mainland legal matters

How specific should a Macau power of attorney for Mainland property be?

Draft authority by task rather than saying 'handle everything'. Record searches, filings, requisitions, collection, negotiation, receipt of money and sale are different levels.

Last updated: 2026-08-18

Three practical moves

  • List the three to five acts needed now.
  • Separate sale, pricing, receipt, sub-delegation and waiver decisions.
  • State the property identifier, term, originals and return process.

Start with the actual decision

Confirm the local recipient's form and remote-handling requirements, then grant enough authority for the present stage without unnecessary control.
