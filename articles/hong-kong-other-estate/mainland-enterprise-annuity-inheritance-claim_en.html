Mainland estates for Hong Kong families

Claiming a Mainland enterprise-annuity balance after death

A Hong Kong family may not know whether a deceased relative had an enterprise annuity. Ask the former employer or plan administrator for the account, vested balance and beneficiary designation. This is separate from survivor benefits under basic pension insurance.

Published: 2026-10-23

Start with the former employer and plan administrator

Provide death, relationship and claimant identity records and ask whether the deceased joined a plan, who administers it, what balance remains and what the plan says. A bank statement may not reveal an annuity managed separately.

Check vesting and beneficiary arrangements

The enterprise-annuity rules address payment of eligible individual-account funds, but the plan, vesting status and payment stage matter. Verify a designated beneficiary, statutory heirs and any annuity insurance purchased at retirement separately.

Ask the administrator for a complete document list

Request the application form, death and relationship evidence, beneficiary information, payment destination and whether a notarised inheritance certificate or final court document is needed. Hong Kong applicants should confirm document format and authority requirements, keeping receipts and the final account statement.

Documents and points to check

  • Former employer and possible plan membership.
  • Individual account or benefit statement.
  • Beneficiary designation and payment status.
  • Death, relationship and claimant identity records.
  • The administrator’s document list, authority rules and settlement receipt.
