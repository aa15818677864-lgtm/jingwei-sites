Mainland estates for Hong Kong families

Using Hong Kong inheritance documents in Mainland China: verify the document and transfer route

For a Mainland estate, a family-status declaration, renunciation, power of attorney and Hong Kong court document serve different purposes and follow different routes. Ask the Mainland receiving institution what must be proved, then prepare the matching document through the appropriate Hong Kong notarial process.

Published: 2026-10-23

Ask the Mainland recipient what fact must be proved

Explain the task, asset location, heirs, whether a will exists and whether anyone renounces. The recipient may need a family-and-will declaration, renunciation, authority or court document; a generic request for an inheritance notarisation is not specific enough.

Confirm the notary and transfer steps

The Hong Kong Association of China-Appointed Attesting Officers lists declarations for inheriting or renouncing Mainland estates. Confirm whether review, sealing and transfer by China Legal Services (Hong Kong) is required, and whether the current process is paper or electronic.

Match each document to the actual application

Check names, identity numbers, family links, deceased details, asset location, authority and expiry. After completion in Hong Kong, keep the reference and Mainland receipt. Correct any mismatch before the file reaches the notary, registry or bank.

Documents and points to check

  • Mainland asset location and receiving institution.
  • The specific fact and document format required.
  • Death, identity, family and will records.
  • Whether a renunciation or power of attorney is needed.
  • Review, transfer, reference number and receipt.
