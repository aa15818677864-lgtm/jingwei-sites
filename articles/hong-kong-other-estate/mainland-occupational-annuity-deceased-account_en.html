Mainland estates for Hong Kong families

Claiming a Mainland occupational-annuity balance after death

Occupational annuities are common for public-institution and government employees, but families may confuse them with basic pension benefits or enterprise annuities. Confirm plan membership, the individual balance and payment stage, then follow the employer’s and administering region’s process.

Published: 2026-10-24

Confirm the plan and administering region

Check retirement records, pay slips, benefit statements and the former employer’s HR office. Transfers between regions or retirement can involve different administrators. Start with the last participating employer to identify the correct office.

Check whether the account was in service or already paying benefits

Official guidance states that an occupational-annuity account balance may be inherited after death in service or after retirement. The calculation still depends on account and contract records, including monthly payments or an annuity-insurance purchase. Future payments are not automatically a cash balance.

Confirm the payment route in writing

Ask the administrator for the account calculation, claim form and accepted death, identity, relationship and authority records. Where there are several heirs, confirm whether payment goes to a designated beneficiary or legally established heirs before anyone divides funds privately.

Documents and points to check

  • Former employer, plan region and retirement status.
  • Occupational-annuity account statement.
  • Any benefits already paid or annuity insurance purchased.
  • Beneficiary, heir and relationship evidence.
  • Written payment route and document list from the administrator.
