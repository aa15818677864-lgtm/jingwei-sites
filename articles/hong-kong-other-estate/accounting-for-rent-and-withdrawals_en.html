Rent and withdrawals continued for years: reconstruct the Mainland estate account first

Start with a transaction reconstruction, not a lump-sum accusation. Separate rent, withdrawals, necessary expenses, advances, distributions and unexplained items around the date of death.

Last updated: 2026-08-18

Three practical moves

  • Align leases, tenant payments, bank statements and property records month by month.
  • Separate taxes, repairs, care, funeral costs and personal spending.
  • Send a neutral reconciliation schedule and split agreed items from disputed ones.

Start with the actual decision

Receiving money does not prove misappropriation, and paying expenses does not create an automatic right of set-off. Each entry needs date, payer, recipient, purpose and supporting record.

Separate confirmed facts, open questions and the outcome the family or business wants. That allows the Mainland handler to decide whether investigation, document preparation, negotiation, registration or a formal procedure comes next.
