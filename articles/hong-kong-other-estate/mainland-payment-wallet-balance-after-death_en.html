Mainland estates for Hong Kong families

A Mainland payment wallet has a balance: how heirs can identify and claim assets

A payment account, wallet balance, in-app investment and private messages are different kinds of interests. A Hong Kong family should not log in using the deceased’s credentials. Contact the platform through official channels to identify assets, debts and claimable items, separating financial value from private content.

Published: 2026-10-24

Separate money, financial products and account access

A withdrawable wallet balance or investment product differs from the account itself, messages or personal-data access. Platform terms may restrict account transfer. Kinship alone does not grant a password or access to all private information.

Request an asset review through official support

Prepare death, relationship and claimant identity records and any platform-specific documents. Use official customer support, estate procedures or a valid court document. Ask how the balance, investments, unsettled transactions, automatic charges and loan debts are handled separately.

Protect data and third-party privacy

Account content may include the deceased’s and others’ private information. Limit a request to estate-related balances and products rather than unrelated messages. Keep support references, documents, decisions and payment records; account closure does not by itself establish that the balance is zero.

Documents and points to check

  • Platform name, phone number or identity clues.
  • Death, family relationship and claimant identity documents.
  • Available wallet, investment and payment records.
  • Official support ticket and written requirements.
  • The requested financial scope and privacy safeguards.
