How Is the Mainland Small-Deposit Limit Calculated Across Several Accounts and Products at One Bank?

Do not assess a simplified route by looking at one passbook or account. The notice ties the limit to the same banking legal entity and includes only specified products. Inventory the assets and ask the bank to calculate the deceased person’s position as a whole.

Published: 2026-10-23

Several accounts do not create separate limits

A person may have current and fixed deposits, several cards, or funds divided among accounts at one bank. The simplified procedure looks at the aggregate balance held with the same legal banking entity, not a fresh limit for every passbook or branch. Different banks are generally assessed separately, but confirm whether branches and products belong to the same entity.

The 2024 notice raised the limit to RMB 50,000 or the foreign-currency equivalent, excluding accrued interest not yet settled. Before applying, confirm the applicant’s eligibility, the bank-level aggregation, and the evidence required. Do not assume that every case qualifies for a notarisation-free procedure based only on an online summary.

Some products count, but not every investment

The notice names bank gold accumulation products and government bonds or wealth-management products sold by a bank as agent. They may be handled after maturity or redemption, and principal plus actual returns count toward the limit. The bank should assist with a redemption or non-trading transfer request to the product manager where appropriate.

This does not put every investment into the simplified route. Funds, securities, trusts, insurance, or products managed elsewhere may have separate succession or transfer procedures. Give the bank each product name, manager, status, and maturity date. Ask which items it aggregates and which items need a separate application.

Prepare an inventory before asking for a calculation

List known bank names, account endings, passbooks, product certificates, maturity notices, and recent statements. If the account picture is incomplete, first ask how information can be searched. A transaction inquiry and withdrawal request are separate; locating an account does not authorise one heir to collect all of its funds.

Even if the bank says the total appears to fit, confirm who must attend, whether a representative may apply, how Hong Kong records should be prepared, and whether a product must first mature or be redeemed. If the total exceeds the limit or heirs disagree, succession rights and allocation need separate attention. Maturity can affect when an application is practical. Do not rely only on a current estimated value to decide whether the family is within the limit. If several products are near maturity, ask the bank what documents are needed for redemption, settlement, or a non-trading transfer, and which institution handles each step. This helps the family avoid treating an asset managed by another institution as an ordinary deposit.

Inventory at the same bank

  • Confirm the bank entity, branches, and account clues
  • Record principal, unsettled interest, and foreign-currency balances
  • Identify product managers and maturity dates
  • Ask about eligibility, aggregation, and Hong Kong records
