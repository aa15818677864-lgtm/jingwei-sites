Mainland estates for Hong Kong families

Registering inherited Mainland fund units: accounts, documents and simplified procedures

Inherited fund units may not need to be sold before they are divided. A fund manager or registration agency may offer an inheritance-related non-trade transfer. First identify the sales channel and registrar and whether each recipient needs an account.

Published: 2026-10-23

Locate the fund account and registrar

Review account cards, confirmations, statements and sales-platform records to identify each fund code, unit balance, distributor and registrar. Forms and account requirements differ across funds and registration systems.

Ask for the inheritance transfer document list

Common requirements may include succession evidence, proof of death, heir identification, an application and recipient accounts. Confirm current requirements with the manager and registrar. Multiple heirs should agree on unit allocations before filing so the application matches the agreement or legal document.

Check whether a simplified small-estate process applies

Some institutions publish simplified procedures for small investor estates, with limits on value, products and eligible applicants. If units cannot be redeemed, a non-trade transfer may be needed. Check each condition; do not assume quick payment is guaranteed.

Documents and points to check

  • Fund codes, unit balances and registrar information.
  • Succession, death and heir identity documents.
  • Fund-account or recipient-account opening rules.
  • Allocation documents for multiple heirs.
  • Value and product limits for any simplified process.
