How Do You Register an Inherited Car in Mainland China? First Decide Which Heir Takes It

For an inherited Mainland vehicle, identify which heir receives it before applying for transfer registration. The succession document, vehicle records, and proof of source required by the police all matter. Local plate and purchase-eligibility rules require separate confirmation.

Published: 2026-10-23

Confirm ownership and the heir arrangement

Check the registered owner on the vehicle licence and registration certificate. Confirm whether the car belonged to the deceased, was jointly owned, or is mortgaged. If there is a will, see whether it covers the vehicle. For intestate succession, identify the heirs and determine whether they agree on who should receive the car.

Several heirs do not each obtain a separate registration. The family normally needs an inheritance agreement, notarial document, or effective court record identifying who takes the car. Ask the vehicle office which succession documents it accepts. Oral family consent may not establish the transfer for registration.

Prepare transfer and succession documents

The Motor Vehicle Registration Provisions require the new owner’s identity, proof of transfer, registration certificate, and vehicle licence, and require the vehicle to be presented as specified. Article 90 of Ministry of Public Security Order No. 164 identifies the source documents for an inherited car as relevant inheritance records and a notarial certificate. Local police guidance may add appointment and inspection details.

Ask the registration office which Hong Kong death, relationship, and authorisation records it accepts. If a Hong Kong notarial instrument is required for use in Mainland China, it is generally issued by a China-Appointed Attesting Officer and reviewed and transmitted by China Legal Services (Hong Kong) Limited. Confirm the document type, translation, and original-document requirements with the vehicle office. If the registration certificate is missing, the vehicle is mortgaged or seized, or inspection has expired, ask whether the issue can be handled with the transfer and who may apply.

Check the vehicle and plate separately

Vehicle ownership and a plate number are different rights. Inheriting a car does not itself establish that the plate may be retained. Ask the local traffic authority whether the old number can remain or a new one must be assigned. Cities such as Beijing and Shanghai may have separate purchase qualifications, quotas, or indicators.

Before filing, check mortgage, seizure, unresolved traffic violations, inspection, and insurance. Confirm whether the vehicle must be presented. A Hong Kong heir may ask a Mainland relative to help, but first confirm accepted authorisation. If the vehicle was registered jointly with a spouse or another person, confirm the registration and actual ownership before treating the entire car as part of the deceased person’s estate. A loan or court restriction may affect the transfer route. Before travelling to the vehicle office, provide the vehicle identification number, registration-certificate number, plate, and registration location and ask whether the record shows an issue that must be addressed first.

Before registering an inherited car

  • Registration certificate, vehicle licence, location, and co-owners
  • Will, inheritance agreement, notarial or court record
  • Mortgage, seizure, inspection, traffic, and insurance status
  • Local requirements for attendance, inspection, and Hong Kong records
