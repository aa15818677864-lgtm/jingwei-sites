A Mainland Vehicle Is Part of the Estate: What Should the Family Check Before Transfer or Sale?

Keys and a registration card do not give someone authority to drive away or sell the vehicle. Match the physical car, registration, liabilities and the family's decision first.

Last updated：2026-08-03

Three points to keep in view

  • Locate the car and all documents
  • Check finance, inspection, insurance and costs
  • Choose the recipient before transfer or sale

Secure the vehicle and its condition before allocating it

A vehicle depreciates and may continue to incur parking, maintenance and insurance costs. Confirm where it is, who holds it and whether it is still being used, then photograph its condition, mileage and identification details.

Match the physical vehicle to the registered holder, registration papers, purchase records and finance documents before deciding what is missing.

Make a physical handover record

Photograph all sides, mileage, identification number location, existing damage, keys and items inside. Record the parking location, custodian, last use and any second key or connected account.

Do not let several relatives use the car. If it must be moved, confirm a suitable method and keep the cost record.

Check registration and purchase records

Find the vehicle registration document, driving permit, purchase invoice or contract, tax and insurance records. If something is missing, record who last held it rather than creating an informal substitute.

If the vehicle is registered to the deceased but was funded or used by a company, spouse or relative, preserve those payment and use arrangements separately.

Check finance, inspection, insurance and outstanding costs

List any loan, mortgage or pledge, inspection expiry, insurance expiry, accident, parking fee or other unsettled item with its date.

If a fine or claim arrives, verify the driver, event date and documents before a relative personally accepts the entire amount.

Decide whether to retain, sell or hold

If one person takes the vehicle, record the valuation date, balancing payment, responsibility for costs and transfer deadline. For a sale, confirm who can sign, where the proceeds go and how any finance will be cleared.

A temporary custodian does not become the owner. Keep separate records of fuel, repairs and parking.

Ask the vehicle authority a complete set of questions

Requirements may depend on the city and vehicle status. Explain the death, applicant, finance, inspection and insurance, then ask whether the vehicle must be presented, which entitlement records are required and whether an agent may act.

Keep the response, appointment, inspection and document list together to reduce repeat travel from Hong Kong.
