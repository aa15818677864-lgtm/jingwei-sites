A Relative Worked in Mainland China: What Payments May Still Need to Be Traced After Death?

Start with the last employer and human-resources contact, but do not call every possible payment employer compensation. Some items are settled by the company and others by separate administrators.

Last updated：2026-08-03

Three points to keep in view

  • Build a final-employment settlement list
  • Separate employer payments, accounts and survivor benefits
  • Ask HR, payroll and administrators distinct questions

Ask for an itemised settlement, not simply whether money is available

Possible items include unpaid salary, an earned bonus or commission, approved expenses, deposits, an occupational account or a survivor benefit that the employer helps report. Not every employee will have every item.

Ask for an itemised statement showing the payer, amount, calculation period and the identity required from the applicant.

Start with the last employment records

Find the employment contract, staff card, payslips, tax or payment records, expense claims, company email and final working date. If there were several employers, separate them by period and identify the actual employing entity.

Record the full company name, city, HR and payroll contacts and the normal salary account. These are leads, not a promise that a payment exists.

Separate three types of payment

The employer may directly settle salary, an earned bonus, expenses or a deposit. A separate administrator may hold a personal account balance. A survivor benefit may be payable under different conditions and may not follow the same distribution as estate property.

Who pays, why the amount arose and who can receive it determine the required documents.

Request a written settlement schedule

Ask HR or payroll for the period, gross and net amount, whether payment already went to the deceased person's account, any company property to return and the department or external administrator handling each item.

After a phone call, keep the date and department and confirm the points by email. Do not surrender the only original document.

Trace the relevant account and administrator

Social insurance, housing fund and occupational arrangements can be managed by different cities or institutions. The employer can provide clues without being the final payer.

Ask for the account location, personal reference, final contribution month and service channel before preparing death, identity, relationship or representative documents.

Clarify the recipient's role before payment

A family contact is not automatically entitled to every item. If one receiving account is requested, ask whether the person is collecting for others, receiving personally or completing the settlement for all relevant family members.

Keep the settlement schedule, payment notice and actual receipt so the family can later distinguish estate property, a specific survivor benefit and money collected on behalf of others.
