Can a Mainland housing allowance be inherited? Check the entitlement and ownership history

A housing allowance may still be under employer review, unpaid or already credited to an account. Ask the former employer for the policy basis, approved amount and payment date, then determine whether it was the deceased’s acquired property, marital property or an entitlement that had not yet vested.

Published: 2026-10-23

Identify the governing allowance scheme

Request the employer’s scheme, eligibility review, calculation and approval records. Conditions differ by employer and period. A shorthand on a payslip or a colleague’s payment does not establish that the deceased had acquired the same amount.

Determine whether a property right had accrued

Check whether the allowance was approved, paid or credited before death and whether housing status, service years or other conditions remained. If the entitlement arose during marriage, assess the acquisition date and marital-property position before calculating the estate.

Ask the employer to confirm the claim route in writing

Request the claimant, documents, receiving account and deadline, distinguishing the allowance from housing-fund or pension balances. If heirs disagree, ask how disputed amounts are held and retain every calculation, notice and accounting record.

Documents and points to check

  • The housing-allowance scheme and applicable version.
  • Employer eligibility review, approved amount and date.
  • Payment, account credit or unpaid status.
  • Marriage and household-housing records.
  • The employer’s written claimant, document and deadline instructions.
