Key point first

When a Hong Kong family finds an unpublished novel, video or design file on a Mainland computer or cloud account, the first question is not simply whether it may be released. Separate authorship, custody of the original file, and any existing publishing or licence arrangement. Succession documents may support transfer of economic rights, but they do not create the author’s intention or automatically authorise publication.

First question to settle

Preserve the original file, versions, creation dates, emails and co-creator information. Do not rename or overwrite the cloud copy first. Commissioned work, a joint work or an employee-created work may follow a different rights chain. Ask the Mainland receiving party whether it needs a rights record, succession material, a licence or only evidence preservation. See IP records and purpose-specific documents.
